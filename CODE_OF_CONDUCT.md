# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in 402 Index respectful, focused, and productive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be specific and technical when disagreeing.
- Assume good intent, but make risks and trade-offs clear.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.
- Respect maintainers' time and contributors' boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publicly posting private information.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Bad-faith reporting, trolling, or attempts to derail security disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block accounts, or take other reasonable action to protect the project. Security issues should be reported through `SECURITY.md`; conduct issues may be sent to `hello@402index.io`.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in this repository, project-managed communication channels, and public interactions where someone is representing the project.
